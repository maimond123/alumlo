## CRITICAL DESIGN REQUIREMENT

### PROPER NOUN EXTRACTION
The fine-tuned model must be able to **extract specific names directly from queries**:
- **Company Names**: Google, Amazon, McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, etc.
- **University Names**: Harvard, Stanford, MIT, Georgia Tech, etc. 
- **Locations**: San Francisco, New York, Atlanta, California, etc.
- **Job Titles**: Director, VP, Manager, Engineer, etc.

These should be extracted as-is and NOT included in mapped_terms. Only categorical terms (industry, function, level, size, degree, ranking, major) should appear in mapped_terms.

## SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE CONTEXT

### Search Pipeline Flow
1. **Input**: Raw natural language query + target_company context
2. **LLM Mapping**: Standardize terms using DATABASE_TERM_MAPPINGS ← **REFERENCE BELOW**
3. **Fine-tuned Translation**: Convert to chronological JSON filters ← **YOUR FOCUS**
4. **SQL Execution**: Query chronological_search_function_demo()

### Database Function Overview
The `chronological_search_function_demo()` function analyzes career and education events across time, building timeline patterns and calculating progression metrics. It differs from standard search by focusing on:
- Career sequence analysis
- Education-career overlap patterns  
- Geographic mobility tracking
- Leadership progression timeline
- Company relationship phases (pre/during/post target company)

### TARGET COMPANY HANDLING

**Critical Context**: The chronological search system is designed around a specific "target company" (e.g., Chick-fil-A) that serves as the temporal anchor for career analysis. The system tracks:
- **Pre-company experience**: Careers before joining the target company
- **During-company experience**: Work at the target company itself
- **Post-company experience**: Careers after leaving the target company

**In Training Data Generation**:
- The target company will be provided as context in the fine-tuning prompt
- When generating examples, assume "Chick-fil-A" is the target company
- Career phase filters (has_pre_company_experience, etc.) relate to this target company
- Company relationship analysis is always relative to Chick-fil-A

**Fine-tuning Input Format**:
```
User: "Target Company: Chick-fil-A
Original Query: [query]
Standardized Query: [mapped query]
Mapped Terms: {...}
Translate to chronological filters."
```

```
**SEARCH TYPE: CHRONOLOGICAL**

**YOUR MISSION:**
1. Identify terms that need standardization using the DATABASE_TERM_MAPPINGS
2. Replace natural language variations with exact database terms
3. Return the standardized query + detailed mapping information
4. Focus on terms relevant to chronological search patterns

**CHRONOLOGICAL SEARCH FOCUS:**
- Career progression patterns and experience depth
- Industry transitions and function changes
- Educational background and career alignment
- Geographic mobility and concurrent activities
- Focus on: industries, functions, levels, degrees, schools, companies

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
Return valid JSON with:
- standardized_query: The query with mapped terms
- mapped_terms: Arrays of all mapped terms by category
- transformations: Detailed log of all mappings made
```

## AVAILABLE FILTERS (Complete Set)

### TEMPORAL-SCOPED FILTERS (NEW - Use these for precise chronological matching)

#### CAREER PHASE FILTERS (Extract company/industry/title/location with temporal context)
- **pre_company_company_filter**: Companies worked at BEFORE target company
- **post_company_company_filter**: Companies worked at AFTER target company  
- **during_company_company_filter**: Companies worked at WHILE at target company
- **pre_company_industry_filter**: Industries worked in BEFORE target company
- **post_company_industry_filter**: Industries worked in AFTER target company
- **during_company_industry_filter**: Industries worked in WHILE at target company
- **pre_company_title_filter**: Job titles held BEFORE target company
- **post_company_title_filter**: Job titles held AFTER target company
- **during_company_title_filter**: Job titles held WHILE at target company
- **pre_company_location_filter**: Locations worked in BEFORE target company
- **post_company_location_filter**: Locations worked in AFTER target company
- **during_company_location_filter**: Locations worked in WHILE at target company
- **pre_company_size_filter**: Company sizes worked at BEFORE target company
- **post_company_size_filter**: Company sizes worked at AFTER target company
- **during_company_size_filter**: Company sizes worked at WHILE at target company

#### EDUCATION PHASE FILTERS (Extract schools with temporal context)
- **pre_company_school_filter**: Schools attended BEFORE target company
- **during_company_school_filter**: Schools attended WHILE at target company
- **post_company_school_filter**: Schools attended AFTER target company

#### EXPERIENCE PHASE FILTERS (Calculate experience within specific time periods)
- **min_pre_company_years**: Minimum years of experience BEFORE target company
- **min_post_company_years**: Minimum years of experience AFTER target company

### LEGACY FILTERS (Backward compatibility - search across all time periods)

#### TEXT FILTERS
- **school_filter**: Educational institutions (any time period)
- **company_filter**: Company names (any time period)
- **industry_filter**: Industry sectors (any time period)
- **title_filter**: Job titles and roles (any time period)
- **location_filter**: Geographic locations (any time period)
- **company_size_filter**: Company size categories (any time period)
- **degree_level_filter**: Education levels (use mapped terms)
- **major_category_filter**: Academic fields of study (use mapped terms)
- **school_type_filter**: Institution types (extract from context)

#### NUMERIC FILTERS
- **total_experience_years**: Minimum total work experience (>=)
- **min_years_in_industry**: Minimum industry-specific experience (>=)
- **target_industry_filter**: Specific industry for min_years_in_industry calculation

#### BOOLEAN PATTERN FILTERS
- **geographic_mobility**: Has worked in multiple locations
- **concurrent_activities**: Overlapping education and career activities

#### ADVANCED CHRONOLOGICAL FILTERS (Boolean)
- **has_leadership_experience**: Has held leadership roles
- **has_management_responsibility**: Has managed teams
- **has_pre_company_experience**: Worked before target company
- **has_post_company_experience**: Worked after target company  
- **worked_at_target_company**: Specifically worked at target company
- **worked_during_company**: Worked elsewhere while at target company
- **educated_before_company**: Educated before target company
- **educated_during_company**: Educated while at target company
- **educated_after_company**: Educated after target company

## CRITICAL: TEMPORAL PRECISION REQUIREMENTS

**The key insight**: Every proper noun (company, school, industry, title, location) should be extracted with explicit temporal context when chronological relationships are mentioned.

### CORRECT vs WRONG Examples:

**Query**: "Cornell grads who worked in hospitality before joining us"

❌ **WRONG** (causes false positives):
```json
{
  "school_filter": "Cornell",
  "industry_filter": "Restaurant/Hospitality",
  "has_pre_company_experience": true
}
```

✅ **CORRECT** (temporally precise):
```json
{
  "pre_company_school_filter": "Cornell",
  "pre_company_industry_filter": "Restaurant/Hospitality"
}
```

**Query**: "Harvard MBAs who moved to tech after leaving"

❌ **WRONG**:
```json
{
  "school_filter": "Harvard",
  "industry_filter": "Technology & Software",
  "has_post_company_experience": true
}
```

✅ **CORRECT**:
```json
{
  "pre_company_school_filter": "Harvard",
  "degree_level_filter": "Master's",
  "major_category_filter": "Business Administration", 
  "post_company_industry_filter": "Technology & Software"
}
```
